V4JB

Atmosphere and texture. Very vibe, very mood.

V4JB is for the long notes: pads, drones, air, fog. You get a bank of sine partials (up to 512 of them), a 12-slot chain that shapes the spectrum, and a morph pad that blends four snapshots of the macros. Sister plugin to TEW03 and K1X, if you already live in that neighbourhood.

It is part of Stupid Audio — five free instruments in one pack. The other four live over there.

GitHub

VST3 and standalone. Additive, a little modular, and not in a hurry.

Edit

Slot 1 builds the spectrum: spacing algorithms, shimmer, spectral noise, a couple of sub octaves. Slots 2–11 are modules you swap in — filters, Hype, Phrase, Wave, rain-on-tin Random Drops, whatever the patch needs. Slot 12 is drive, chorus or phaser, delay, reverb.

The teal bars on each card are a snapshot of the first 64 partials after that slot, once you have played a note. Empty bars just mean the plugin is waiting for you to say hello.

Mod

Four envelopes and three LFOs. Env 1 is always the loudness of the note; the others exist to be routed. Each module slot can grab one envelope, one LFO, and one MIDI source for its main knob. That is the whole routing story, and it is enough.

Morph

Eleven macros, an XY pad, four corners. Store what you like into A, B, C, D, then wander between them. Drag the puck by hand, send it around a drawn path, or let each new note sweep the X axis like a slow envelope that also happens to be pretty.

Wave

Two files, A and B. Nothing here makes a sound on its own — drop a Wave or Double Wave module on Edit and the partials follow the file. Built-ins (bells, chords, rain, vowels) or your own wav / aiff / flac / ogg / mp3.

Click V4JB in the corner for About, if you need a version number or a polite check for updates.

Download V4JB

The Windows installer is the easy path. It can put the VST3, the standalone app, or both on the machine, and it adds a Start menu shortcut for the app.

Windows installer

The VST3 zip is just the plugin. Drop it where your DAW looks for VST3s. Mac builds are ad-hoc signed, so Gatekeeper will sulk until you right-click Open the first time.

Windows Linux macOS

The standalone zip is the app, no DAW required. Same Gatekeeper story on Mac. Linux wants the usual system ALSA, FreeType, fontconfig, and X11 libraries.
