# V4JB – Manual

## Install

Run `V4JB-<version>-win-x64.msi`. Pick VST3, Standalone, or both. Defaults are `Common Files\VST3` and `Program Files\Stupid Systems LLC\V4JB`.

Or unzip `V4JB-<version>-win-x64-vst3.zip` into your VST3 folder.

## Controls

Hold a MIDI note to play. The note sets the pitch of the acid squelch; the newest held note wins. In the Standalone, pick your MIDI keyboard under Options > Audio/MIDI Settings.

- **Motion**: how fast and far the sound sweeps, rises and falls, and whirls between left and right.
- **Crackle**: cracks and pops. Off at 0, a crackling fire at 100%.
- **Acid**: random squelchy blurts with a resonant, driven filter.
- **Air**: wind and noise at 100%, buzzy saw pad at 0%.
- **Volume** (top right): output level.
- **V4JB / version** (top left): click for About. "Check for update" asks GitHub for a newer tag.

Defaults are soft fog. Turn everything up for whirlwinds, pops and acid farts.
