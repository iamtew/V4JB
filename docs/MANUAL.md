# V4JB – Manual

## Install

Run `V4JB-<version>-win-x64.msi`. Pick VST3, Standalone, or both. Defaults are `Common Files\VST3` and `Program Files\Stupid Systems LLC\V4JB`.

Or unzip `V4JB-<version>-win-x64-vst3.zip` into your VST3 folder.

## Controls

Hold a MIDI note to play. The note sets the pitch of the acid squelch; the newest held note wins. In the Standalone, pick your MIDI keyboard under Options > Audio/MIDI Settings. The Standalone window also has piano keys along the bottom (C2 to C5); click or drag them.

- **Motion**: how fast and far the sound sweeps, rises and falls, and whirls between left and right.
- **Crackle**: cracks and pops. Off at 0, a crackling fire at 100%.
- **Acid**: random squelchy blurts with a resonant, driven filter.
- **Air**: wind and noise at 100%, buzzy saw pad at 0%.

The second row is the ghost choir: up to three voices singing or whispering vowels inside the fog, on your note.

- **Ghost**: off at 0. One faint voice in the middle first, then two more join on the left and right.
- **Vowel**: what they sing, from a dark "oo" through "oh", "ah", "eh" to a bright "ee".
- **Breath**: sung at 0%, a breathy whisper at 100%.
- **Haunt**: how much the vowels wander, how far out of tune the voices drift from each other, and how much they swell in and out. At 100% a ghost can fade away completely for a moment.

- **Volume** (top right): output level.
- **V4JB / version** (top left): click for About. "Check for update" asks GitHub for a newer tag.

Defaults are soft fog. Turn everything up for whirlwinds, pops and acid farts. Turn up Ghost for something singing out there.
